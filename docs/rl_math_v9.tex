\item the \textbf{v9 observation} (notebook \cd{setup1\_v9\_a100.ipynb} $=$ v8 $+$ one change):
  ownership becomes a \textbf{4-channel ego-relative seat one-hot} ($F{=}104$) so the policy can

\end{itemize}

\section{$N$-player environment}\label{v9:env}
The board, production, orbits, launches, swept collision, sun occlusion, and official comets are
exactly \rlref{Batched GPU simulator}{sec:gpuenv} with the v6 data path (\vsixref{sys}). v8/v9 add
$N$-player play ($N\in\{2,4\}$) without touching the 2p path.

\paragraph{Seating.} The official engine assigns homes on one random symmetric group of four
planets. \cd{generate\_world} records that group's base index $b$; \cd{reset(worlds, n\_players)}
seats owners exactly like the engine's branches:
\[
N{=}2:\ \ \text{owner}(b)=0,\ \text{owner}(b{+}3)=1;\qquad
N{=}4:\ \ \text{owner}(b{+}j)=j,\ j=0..3;\qquad
S_{\text{home}}=10 .
\]
Under the board's 4-fold rotational symmetry every seat is fair in both formats.

\paragraph{Combat: official top-vs-second, $N$ players.} Arrivals at planet $e$ are summed per
player, $A_e^{(p)}=\sum_{\text{fleets}\to e,\ \text{owner}=p}n$, and the official resolve is the
top-two statistic
\begin{equation}
\text{surv}_e \;=\; A_e^{(1\text{st})}-A_e^{(2\text{nd})},\qquad
\text{owner}^{\text{surv}}_e=\begin{cases}\arg\max_p A_e^{(p)} & \text{surv}_e>0\\[2pt]
\varnothing & \text{tie},\end{cases}
\end{equation}
implemented as a $(B,E,N)$ scatter-add $+$ \cd{topk(2)}. For $N{=}2$ this is \emph{algebraically
identical} to the v6 pairwise max/min resolve; survivors reinforce or attack the garrison exactly as
before. \textbf{Verified}: \cd{scripts/test\_v8\_parity.py} steps v7 and v8 side-by-side 70 ticks
(comet spawn included) with identical actions --- planet/fleet state and encodings bit-exact;
\cd{scripts/test\_v9\_parity.py} repeats v8 vs.\ v9 (state bit-exact; encodings differ only by
\S\ref{v9:obs}, consistently).

\paragraph{Seat interface.} \cd{env\_step(env, a, seats=[\dots])} takes, per opponent pid
$1..N{-}1$, either a scripted code (the v6/v7 \cd{opponent\_action}, now seat-parameterized) or a
neural gated-alloc action decoded exactly like the ego's. Termination: step cap, $\le\!1$ player
alive, or ego death.

\section{Observation: per-seat categorical ownership (the v9 change)}\label{v9:obs}
\texttt{rl\_math.tex} \S\,\texttt{sec:obs} encodes ownership as one scalar body feature
$b_7\in\{0,1,2\}$ (neutral/ego/enemy), computed ego-relatively with $n_a{=}2$ hard-coded. That
scalar cannot represent \emph{which} enemy owns a body, and the formula does not extend: fed a
4-player state, $(o-\mathrm{ego})\bmod 2$ \textbf{aliases seat $\mathrm{ego}{+}2$ onto the ego
code} --- the policy would read an opponent's planets as its own. v8 therefore \emph{collapses}
every non-ego owner to the single code $2$ (the deployed everyone-vs-me adapter view; $F{=}101$,
v7 weights drop in). v9 replaces the scalar entirely.

\paragraph{Seat one-hot.} With $N=\cd{n\_players}$ and ego seat $g$, define the ego-relative seat
index and the 4-channel categorical
\begin{equation}
\rho_e=(o_e-g)\bmod N,\qquad
\mathbf b_{7}[e]=\Big(\underbrace{\mathbf{1}[o_e\!\ge\!0\wedge\rho_e{=}0]}_{\text{self}},\;
\mathbf{1}[o_e\!\ge\!0\wedge\rho_e{=}1],\;
\mathbf{1}[o_e\!\ge\!0\wedge\rho_e{=}2],\;
\mathbf{1}[o_e\!\ge\!0\wedge\rho_e{=}3]\Big),
\label{v9:onehot}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ $[\,\text{self}\,|\,\text{enemy seat}{+}1\,|\,{+}2\,|\,{+}3\,]$ with \textbf{neutral $=$
all-zero}. $N_{\text{body}}\,11\!\to\!14$, so $F=101\to104$. Properties that motivated the design:
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \textbf{categorical, not ordinal}: the scalar $2..n_a$ code imposes a fake magnitude and
  ordering across seats; one-hot channels are permutation-symmetric inputs the network can attach
  independent behavior to (per-enemy threat assessment, target selection);
\item \textbf{rotation-equivariant}: the same \emph{relative} seat always lands in the same
  channel, from any ego seat (verified: ego $0$ sees owners $1,2,3$ as $0100/0010/0001$; ego $2$
  sees itself as $1000$ and the others rotated);
\item \textbf{format-generalizing}: in 2p only the first two channels ever fire, so the 4p input
  space is a strict superset --- and when a seat is eliminated mid-FFA its channel simply stops
  firing, so 3-player intermediate states remain well-formed without ever being trained explicitly;
\item \textbf{no aliasing}: $\rho$ is taken mod $N$ (not mod 2), so no opponent can collide with
  the ego channel.
\end{itemize}
Everything else in the observation is unchanged: the incoming-fleet threat block keeps the
\emph{signed} friend/foe convention ($+1$ ego, $-1$ any opponent), and the global aggregates pool
\emph{all} opponents (enemy ships $=\sum_{p\neq g}$, enemy production share vs.\ the pooled field)
--- seat identity enters only through Eq.~\eqref{v9:onehot}.

\paragraph{Legacy members: the obs adapter.} v9's league still admits v7/v8-format checkpoints
(invited or seeded; their ckpt config carries $F{=}101$). They are wrapped in
\cd{LegacyObsAdapter}, which collapses the one-hot back to the scalar they were trained on before
their forward:
\begin{equation}
b_7^{\text{legacy}} \;=\; \mathbf b_7[0] \;+\; 2\,\min\!\Big(1,\ \textstyle\sum_{k=1}^{3}\mathbf b_7[k]\Big)
\in\{0,1,2\},
\end{equation}
exact on every reachable state (the channels are disjoint indicators). The wrapper is unwrapped on
league-state save and re-wrapped on load; it is dormant in v8 (dimensions match). The \emph{learner}
cannot be warm-started from v7/v8 weights ($F$ changed): v9 runs its own BC warm-start
(\vsevenref{bc}; the BC expert is scripted, so nothing else changes).

\section{Reward: $N$-player potentials and outcome}\label{v9:reward}
The reward family is v6 potential shaping $+$ terminal outcome (\vsixref{reward}), generalized so
that $N{=}2$ reproduces it exactly.

\paragraph{Potentials vs.\ the strongest opponent.} With per-player totals $S^{(p)}$ (planets $+$
fleets), owned production $\Pi^{(p)}$, ship-log $\ell(n)=\ln(1{+}n)/\ln 1000$:
\begin{equation}
\Phi_{\mathrm{ship}}(s)=\ell\big(S^{(0)}\big)-\ell\big(\max_{p\ge1}S^{(p)}\big),\qquad
\Phi_{\mathrm{prod}}(s)=\frac{\Pi^{(0)}-\max_{p\ge1}\Pi^{(p)}}{\max(\Pi_{\mathrm{tot}},1)} .
\end{equation}
The $\max$ is the right opponent aggregate for a \emph{win-the-game} potential: in an FFA you are
measured against whoever currently leads, not the average of the field. For $N{=}2$ both reduce to
the v6 pair. The shaping form $r_t=c\,[\gamma\Phi(s_{t+1})-\Phi(s_t)]$, the coefficients, the
$\sigma_R$ scaling, and the policy-invariance argument are unchanged (\vsixref{reward}).

\paragraph{Pairwise scores and the 4p outcome.} At termination, against each seat $k$ with totals
$S^{(k)}$:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{sc}_k=\begin{cases}1 & S^{(0)}>S^{(k)}\\ \tfrac12 & S^{(0)}=S^{(k)}\\ 0 & S^{(0)}<S^{(k)},\end{cases}
\qquad
\Omega=\begin{cases}
\sign\!\big(S^{(0)}-\max_{p\ge1}S^{(p)}\big) & N{=}2\ \text{or}\ \cd{OUTCOME\_4P}{=}\text{winner},\\[4pt]
2\cdot\frac1{N-1}\sum_k \mathrm{sc}_k\;-\;1 & \cd{OUTCOME\_4P}{=}\text{placement (default)}.
\end{cases}
\label{v9:outcome}
\end{equation}
The placement-linear $\Omega\in\{-1,-\tfrac13,+\tfrac13,+1\}$ maps finishing place affinely
(beat-all $=+1$, lose-to-all $=-1$): a dense, correctly-ordered learning signal where the official
top-score-only rule pays a 4p game like a lottery (2nd of 4 $=$ dead loss). The terminal payment is
the v6 win/loss magnitude scaled \emph{linearly} by $\Omega$,
$O=\Omega\cdot(\Omega>0\,?\,W:\,L)$, so 2p (where $\Omega\in\{\pm1,0\}$) pays exactly as before.
The per-seat $\mathrm{sc}_k$ are also the dual-Elo update signal (\S\ref{v9:elo}); the logged
\cd{win\_rate} in 4p is the strict ``beat \emph{every} opponent'' indicator
$\mathbf{1}[\min_k\mathrm{sc}_k\ge 1]$.

%================================================================
\section{Unified rollout}\label{v9:rollout}
One \cd{collect\_ppo(env, net, pool, cursor, rng, seats, n\_players)} runs both formats. The
learner is always player 0 with the usual on-GPU buffers and GPU GAE (\vsixref{sys}); each opponent
seat is either a scripted anchor (fully on-GPU, zero forwards) or a frozen neural member acting
per-step under \cd{no\_grad} via its own \cd{env\_encode(env, pid)} --- the same encode function,
ego $=$ that seat (Eq.~\eqref{v9:onehot} makes this seat-symmetric by construction). A 4p iteration
therefore costs the learner's rollout $+$ up to three no-grad forwards per step; scripted seats are
free. \cd{B\_4P}$=B$ keeps the learner's transition count format-independent. Termination,
\cd{done}-correct GAE, the outcome scatter, PopArt, and the PPO update are untouched
(\vsixref{ppo}, \vsevenref{guard}).

\section{Dual correlated Elo}\label{v9:elo}
Heads-up strength and FFA strength are correlated but \emph{not} identical (the \cd{it1041}
failure). Every league member --- and the learner --- carries two ratings $(e^{2}, e^{4})$.
Scripted anchors and invited members are \textbf{anchored in both} (their ratings never move);
snapshots float.

\paragraph{2p update.} After a 2p iteration vs.\ member $m$ with mean learner score
$\bar s\in[0,1]$ over the batch, with expectation
$\E=\big(1+10^{(e^2_m-e^2_\ell)/400}\big)^{-1}$:
\begin{equation}
\Delta=K\,(\bar s-\E),\qquad
e^2_\ell \mathrel{+}= \Delta,\quad e^4_\ell \mathrel{+}= \kappa\,\Delta,\qquad
\text{(non-anchor $m$ mirrored: } e^2_m\mathrel{-}=\Delta,\ e^4_m\mathrel{-}=\kappa\Delta\text{)},
\end{equation}
with $K=\cd{ELO\_K}=32$ and the \textbf{cross-format coupling} $\kappa=\cd{ELO\_COUPLING}=0.25$.

\paragraph{4p update: pairwise decomposition.} A 4p FFA vs.\ seats $\{m_k\}$ becomes
$N{-}1$ pairwise updates at $K_4/(N{-}1)$ each (the standard multiplayer decomposition --- total
rating motion per game matches a 2p game at $K_4$):
\begin{equation}
\Delta_k=\frac{K_4}{N-1}\big(\mathrm{sc}_k-\E_k^{(4)}\big),\qquad
e^4_\ell\mathrel{+}=\Delta_k,\quad e^2_\ell\mathrel{+}=\kappa\,\Delta_k\quad\text{for each }k,
\end{equation}
$K_4=\cd{ELO\_K4}=32$, $\E^{(4)}$ the logistic in the $e^4$ ratings. The coupling encodes the
prior that the two skills share most of their variance: a 2p result moves the 4p belief a quarter
as much (and vice versa), keeping the ratings correlated without forcing them equal --- it is
exactly their \emph{gap} that drives the scheduler below.

\paragraph{Recalibration.} The 2p anchor back-solve is unchanged (\vsixref{league}); the 4p analog
re-grounds $e^4$ from the fixed FFA trio: with mean pairwise score $s$ vs.\
$\{\cd{starter},\cd{greedy},\cd{intermediate}\}$ and their mean anchored rating $\bar a$,
$e^4=\bar a+400\log_{10}\!\big(s/(1{-}s)\big)$, $s$ clipped to $[0.02,0.98]$.

%================================================================
\section{Gap-driven match-type mix}\label{v9:mix}
The share of 4p iterations is a bounded affine function of the dual-rating gap:
\begin{equation}
s_4=\clip\!\Big(\,\cd{S4\_BASE}+\cd{S4\_GAIN}\cdot\frac{e^2_\ell-e^4_\ell}{400},\ \cd{S4\_MIN},\ \cd{S4\_MAX}\Big)
=\clip\big(0.60+\text{gap}/400,\ \tfrac12,\ \tfrac67\big).
\label{v9:share}
\end{equation}
When the 4p rating lags, more 4p is scheduled (and vice versa); the bounds are the
\textbf{1:1\,..\,1:6 envelope} --- 4p may dominate up to six 4p iterations per 2p iteration but
never starves either format. The scheduler is \textbf{error-diffused}: an accumulator
$a\mathrel{+}=s_4$ each iteration, playing 4p iff $a\ge1$ (then $a\mathrel{-}=1$), so the
\emph{realized} ratio equals the target exactly over any window (a Bernoulli draw would only match
in expectation, with format droughts at small iteration counts).

\section{League membership: invited members and weak-point matchmaking}\label{v9:members}
PFSP sampling, the behavioral-fingerprint diversity term, snapshot pruning, and the scripted-anchor
boosts are unchanged (\vsixref{league}). 4p seats are $k{=}3$ weighted draws \emph{without}
replacement from the same per-format weights. Two additions:

\paragraph{(a) Invited members.} The first existing folder in \cd{INVITE\_DIRS} is scanned for
\cd{*.pt}; the top \cd{INVITE\_MAX}$=4$ by anchored rating join the league as fixed high-level
bots. The anchor resolves as \emph{filename suffix} \cd{\_elo<NNNN>} $\succ$ ckpt meta \cd{elo}
$\succ$ \cd{INVITE\_DEFAULT\_ELO}$=750$ (the submitted \cd{it1041} leaderboard level), and never
updates. While unmastered they sample at $\times\cd{INVITE\_MATCH\_BOOST}{=}2$ (high-level
sparring focus); once the learner's per-format score EMA passes \cd{INVITE\_MASTER\_WR}$=0.75$
their rate \emph{drops} to $\times\cd{INVITE\_MASTERED\_W}{=}0.2$ --- a small anti-forgetting
floor rather than continued compute spend. Per-member architecture comes from the ckpt's own
config (smaller invited nets are fine); v7/v8-format members get the \S\ref{v9:obs} obs adapter.
Caveat (measured): a ckpt whose \emph{meta} carries \cd{elo:\,0} anchors at 0 --- the default only
applies when the meta key is absent; curate invite folders with \cd{\_elo} suffixes (the league
export writes them).

\paragraph{(b) Weak-point matchmaking.} Per member and per format, compare the learner's
\emph{actual} score EMA $w$ against the Elo-\emph{expected} score $\E$. When reality runs below
theory the member is a measured weak point and its sampling weight is boosted:
\begin{equation}
\beta(m,\text{fmt})=
\begin{cases}
1 & n<\cd{WEAK\_BOOST\_MIN\_GAMES}\ \text{or}\ \E-w<\cd{WEAK\_BOOST\_MIN\_GAP},\\[2pt]
\min\!\big(\cd{WEAK\_BOOST\_MAX},\ 1+\cd{WEAK\_BOOST\_GAIN}\,(\E-w)\big) & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
with $(5,\,0.05,\,10,\,3)$: e.g.\ Elo expects $0.90$ but the EMA reads $0.70$ $\Rightarrow$ gap
$0.20$ $\Rightarrow$ $\times3$ (the cap). It fires \emph{only} on underperformance ---
overperforming opponents are never down-weighted --- and the deadband keeps EMA noise from
triggering it. Active boosts are surfaced on the leaderboard as \cd{weak2/weak4 x<mult>}.

%================================================================
\section{Mixed best-checkpoint gauntlet}\label{v9:gauntlet}
v6 selected ``best'' on the fixed-opponent \emph{2p} gauntlet (\vsixref{league}); that rule is what
crowned \cd{it1041} while it was losing FFAs. v9 selects on the mix it will be graded on:
\begin{equation}
g \;=\; (1-w_4)\,g_2 \;+\; w_4\,g_4,\qquad w_4=\cd{GAUNTLET\_4P\_W}=0.5,
\end{equation}
where $g_2$ is the v6 2p gauntlet (greedy play vs.\ \cd{starter}/\cd{intermediate}/\cd{greedy} on
held-out worlds) and $g_4$ is the mean \emph{pairwise} score (Eq.~\eqref{v9:outcome} left) of a 4p
FFA vs.\ the fixed trio $\{\cd{starter},\cd{greedy},\cd{intermediate}\}$ on the same held-out
worlds. A 2p-only specialist can no longer win the checkpoint race.

\section{Anchor scale: leaderboard-calibrated}\label{v9:anchors}
The scripted anchors were re-pinned from the in-house round-robin scale to the \emph{submitted}
Kaggle leaderboard levels, so the learner's rating reads directly against the competition:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccl@{}}
\toprule
anchor & old & new & basis \\
\midrule
\cd{random}        & 0    & 0   & scale pin \\
\cd{starter}       & 600  & 450 & submitted starter $\approx$ 450 \\
\cd{intermediate}  & 750  & 475 & never submitted; interpolated (keeps in-house ordering) \\
\cd{greedy}        & 900  & 500 & submitted greedy $\approx$ 500 \\
\cd{medium}        & 1000 & 500 & submitted medium $\approx$ 500 \\
\midrule
\cd{ADVANCE\_TRIGGER/CONFIRM} & 800/700 & 525/425 & $\pm50$ around \cd{intermediate}, as before \\
\cd{INVITE\_DEFAULT\_ELO}     & 1100    & 750     & $=$ submitted \cd{it1041} level \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Two consequences to keep in mind. (i) The compressed scale \emph{understates in-house win
probabilities}: starter$\to$medium is a $\sim$90\%-win gap in-house but is now encoded as 50 Elo
($\approx$57\%), so PFSP/weak-boost \emph{expected} scores flatten toward $\tfrac12$ --- the
weak-point detector compensates since it compares against the same $\E$. (ii) League state stores
member ratings: \textbf{resuming a pre-rescale train-state keeps the old anchors}; fresh runs (or
warm-starts without league state) use the new ones.

%================================================================
\section{Deployment constraint}\label{v9:deploy}
The v7-era Kaggle adapters map \emph{every} opponent to the single enemy owner before encoding ---
correct for v7/v8 (it \emph{is} their training view) and \textbf{wrong for v9}: it would zero the
seat channels' information and discard the trained per-enemy behavior. The v9 submission adapter
must \emph{preserve seats}: map each opponent id to its own owner $1..3$ (any fixed assignment ---
Eq.~\eqref{v9:onehot} is ego-relative, so only consistency matters within a game), set
\cd{n\_players}$=4$ in 4p episodes, and $=2$ heads-up. v9 checkpoints ($F{=}104$) do not load in
the v7-era eval tools.

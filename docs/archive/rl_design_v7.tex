\noindent\textbf{How to read this.} This document is a \emph{delta} on top of
\texttt{rl\_math\_v6.tex} (which is itself a delta on \texttt{rl\_math.tex}). The simulator, the
gated-Dirichlet allocation action space, the potential-based reward, PopArt, and the Elo/PFSP league
are all unchanged from v6 and are not restated. v7 answers one question:
\emph{what training method + architecture is demonstrably stable and improvable at small scale on an
RTX~3070~Ti, such that the same recipe can be scaled on an A100 with high confidence?}
The answer has three parts: (i) a \textbf{behavior-cloning (BC) warm-start} that expresses the
strongest scripted bot directly in the gated-allocation action space and eliminates the from-scratch
bootstrap failure entirely; (ii) three \textbf{PPO stability guardrails} (per-minibatch KL hard stop,
linear LR warmup, critic-only warmup) that remove the catastrophic first-update collapse;
(iii) a \textbf{measured compute model} (steps/s, \$/iter, ms/move at deploy) that sizes the A100 run
and proves the deployment budget is nowhere near binding.

\noindent\textbf{Method, in one paragraph.} Train a per-planet-token transformer with two factored
heads --- a per-source \emph{categorical} over destinations (WHERE) and a per-source Bernoulli
launch gate (FIRE) --- by (1) behavior-cloning the strongest scripted bot directly into those heads
(2--3 minutes of GPU time), then (2) guardrailed PPO against the Elo/PFSP league. Every piece of
this was forced by a measured failure of the alternative, documented below.

\section{Why the bootstrap, not the algorithm, was the wall}\label{v7:why}

Every from-scratch run in this project's history --- GRPO (\rlref{GRPO}{sec:grpo}), PPO+GAE, the v5
target actor, the v6 gated allocation --- hit the same two walls, reproduced one final time as a
controlled 30-iteration smoke (256-dim/2-layer transformer, $B{=}64$, $T{=}300$, 3070~Ti):

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Catastrophic first update.} At iteration 1 the measured update KL was $2.06$ with
clip-fraction $0.98$: \emph{every} sample was clipped, i.e.\ the update was pure surrogate-boundary
drift. The launch rate fell from $13.5$ to $1.0$ launches/step in that single iteration --- the
fresh policy's exploratory aggression was annihilated before the critic could attribute credit. The
existing end-of-epoch KL check (\vsixref{ppo}) fires only \emph{after} a full epoch (64 minibatch
optimizer steps) has already run away.
\item \textbf{Passivity plateau.} After the slam, the policy stabilizes at $\sim$1 launch/step,
beats the random anchor ($\approx 0.8$ win rate) and stalls: policy loss $\approx 0$, KL
$\approx 10^{-3}$, and the fixed-opponent gauntlet (starter/intermediate/greedy) reads $0.000$ for
all 30 iterations. This is the same basin documented for v4/v5; reward re-shaping moved which basin
we landed in but never removed it. The best from-scratch result ever obtained here was a $0.47$
win rate vs.\ starter after 1400 iterations ($\approx$\,20\,h on the 3070~Ti).
\end{enumerate}

The conclusion that drives v7: \emph{the optimization machinery is fine; the starting point is
fatal.} The fix is to start PPO from a policy that already commits, aims, and wins against the
scripted ladder --- which the gated-allocation action space makes trivially expressible
(\S\ref{v7:bc}) --- and to make the first PPO updates structurally incapable of destroying it
(\S\ref{v7:guard}).

\section{Gradient-vanish fix: verified}\label{v7:grad}

v6's gradient collapse was caused by applying the deploy-time gate deadzone
$p = \clip\!\big((\sigma(z)-0.2)/0.6,\,0,\,1\big)$ during training: $\clip$ has zero gradient
outside the band, and as gate logits sharpen, both the gate head and (through the
$\mathrm{fire}\cdot\log\pi_{\text{where}}$ coupling) the WHERE head starve. The fix (already in the
notebook, \cd{GatedAllocDist(deploy=False)}: training uses smooth $p=\sigma(z)$ clamped to
$[\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]$; deployment keeps the hard trim; the greedy fire boundary
$p\ge 0.5 \Leftrightarrow z\ge 0$ is identical in both) is now \textbf{empirically verified}: over
the 30-iteration smoke the gradient norm stayed in $[0.7,\,5.4]$ with no downward trend, where the
pre-fix run decayed monotonically toward zero.

\section{BC warm-start in the gated-allocation space}\label{v7:bc}

\subsection{The expert, expressed natively in the action space}
The strongest scripted anchor is \cd{medium} (nearest non-owned planet, lead-aimed, fire when
$\lfloor s/2\rfloor \ge 20$). Its decision structure maps \emph{exactly} onto the two gated-alloc
heads --- this is the payoff of the v6 action-space design:
\[
\text{fire}^\ast_{s} = \mathbb{1}\big[\text{owned}_s \wedge \text{alive}_s \wedge
\lfloor S_s/2\rfloor \ge 20 \wedge \exists\,\text{target}\big],
\qquad
\text{where}^\ast_{s} = \operatorname{onehot}\big(\arg\min_{d\,:\,\text{non-owned}} \|x_d - x_s\|\big).
\]
Two deliberate deviations from the literal bot: (i) the gated decode dispatches the \emph{full}
garrison on fire (the action space cannot express ``send half''), so the clone is
\textbf{``full-send medium''} --- measured \emph{stronger} than its teacher (Table~\ref{tab:bc}),
consistent with the project-wide lesson that expansion-race aggression dominates; (ii) expert
targets occluded by the sun are dropped from the WHERE loss (the clone learns
``medium minus sun-suicide'').

\subsection{Loss: native to the distribution, not a surrogate}
With $p_s$ the smooth gate fire-probability and $\ell_{sd}$ the masked WHERE logits (alive +
reachability + no-self, the same \cd{glogits} the sharpened-softmax greedy uses):
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BC}}
= \underbrace{-\tfrac{1}{|O|}\sum_{s\in O}\Big[\text{fire}^\ast_s\log p_s + (1-\text{fire}^\ast_s)\log(1-p_s)\Big]}_{\text{gate BCE, all owned sources}}
\;\underbrace{-\tfrac{1}{|F|}\sum_{s\in F}\log\softmax(\ell_{s\cdot})_{d^\ast_s}}_{\text{WHERE CE, firing sources only}} .
\]
The CE drives $\ell_{sd^\ast}$ up exactly where $\alpha = \softplus(\ell)\,\kappa$ concentrates the
training-time Dirichlet \emph{and} where the deploy-time sharpened softmax looks, so supervised and
RL phases optimize the same parameters with consistent semantics. No new heads, no adapter.

\subsection{Data: states visited by the clone itself}
Rollouts are generated by the \emph{expert acting in the clone's own action space} (one-hot bundles
through the standard decode) against a starter-heavy scripted mix, so the state distribution BC fits
is the distribution the warm-started policy will actually visit --- a free DAgger-like property; no
compounding-error gap on the states that matter. Post-terminal states are masked out; encoded states
are stashed on CPU ($\sim$0.6\,GB per 500-step rollout at $B{=}64$).

\section{WHERE becomes a categorical (the v7 architecture decision)}\label{v7:cat}

\subsection{The measured failure of PPO on the Dirichlet}
Warm-started PPO with the v6 Dirichlet WHERE head \emph{destroys} the BC policy. With all
guardrails of \S\ref{v7:guard} active (run \cd{runs/v6/ppoS2}), the fixed-opponent gauntlet decayed
$0.755 \to 0.625 \to 0.276 \to 0.104$ over 40 iterations while the per-update $k_3$ KL spiked to
$2$--$9$ \emph{despite} a per-minibatch KL hard stop. The mechanism is intrinsic, not a tuning
artifact: BC concentrates $\alpha=\softplus(\ell)\kappa$ into near-one-hot rows, and the log-density
of a 48-dimensional Dirichlet near a simplex vertex is so steep that a \emph{single} clipped,
LR-warmed optimizer step moves stored-action log-probs by whole nats. PPO's ratio clip bounds each
sample's loss contribution, and the KL stop bounds damage per \emph{epoch} --- nothing bounds damage
per \emph{step} when the density itself is this sharp. (The same property produced the unbounded
negative Dirichlet entropy of \S\ref{v7:ent}.)

\subsection{The replacement}
The WHERE head keeps its masked logits $\ell_{sd}$ (alive + sun-reachability + no-self,
\vsixref{dist}) but the distribution over them becomes a per-source \textbf{categorical};
the sampled action row is the \emph{one-hot} of the drawn destination, so the action bundle
$(B,E,E{+}1)$, the decode, the rollout buffers, the league fingerprint, and the checkpoints are all
untouched. Three independent arguments select this design:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Numerics.} $\log\softmax(\ell)_{d}$ has bounded sensitivity in $\ell$; PPO on
factored categoricals is its most battle-tested regime. Measured: first-update KL drops from
$0.53$--$2.06$ (Dirichlet, from scratch) to $0.008$--$0.010$ (categorical, same setup).
\item \textbf{Deploy equivalence.} The v6 \emph{deployed} policy already collapses the Dirichlet to
a temperature-sharpened softmax over the same $\ell$ --- effectively one-hot. The continuous
``split allocation'' expressivity was paid for in training fragility and never used at deploy; the
gate-as-rate (fire probability over repeated steps, garrison refilled by production) already
delivers multi-target pressure over time.
\item \textbf{Search-readiness.} A fully discrete factored action (categorical WHERE $\times$
Bernoulli FIRE) is the prerequisite for the planned Gumbel-AlphaZero extension
(\rlref{search-based policy improvement}{sec:search}); the Dirichlet was the blocker.
\end{enumerate}
The BC loss of \S\ref{v7:bc} is \emph{exactly} the categorical log-likelihood, so the same BC
checkpoint warm-starts both variants; \cd{WHERE\_DIST='dirichlet'} retains the old path for A/B.

\subsection{Attribution: what the Dirichlet did and did not cause}\label{v7:attrib}
A follow-up $2{\times}2$ (from-scratch $\times$ \{categorical, Dirichlet\}, both under the full
\S\ref{v7:guard} guardrails, 30 iters, 256/L2) plus a low-LR BC leg separates three phenomena that
were previously conflated:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
phenomenon & cause & evidence \\
\midrule
\textbf{bootstrap wall} (can't beat starter & \emph{not} the distribution: & scratch-cat gauntlet 0.083, Elo 170; \\
\quad from scratch in reasonable time) & credit assignment + opponent gap & scratch-dir 0.016, Elo 125 (both walled) \\
\textbf{first-update slam} (launch rate & Dirichlet $\times$ \emph{unguarded} update; & old code: it1 KL 2.06, lnch 13.5$\to$1.0; \\
\quad annihilated at it1) & guardrails alone remove it & guardrailed dir: KL $\le$0.024, no slam \\
\textbf{post-BC erosion} & Dirichlet log-density sharpness; & LR $10^{-4}$: gauntlet 0.755$\to$0.104; \\
 & LR-dependent (trust-region misfit) & LR $10^{-5}$: 0.719$\to$0.771 (survives) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\noindent So the bootstrap wall is \emph{distribution-independent} (it also predates the Dirichlet:
the v5 actor's destination head was already categorical and still needed 1400 iterations for a
0.47 starter win rate); BC removes it under \emph{either} distribution. The Dirichlet's real crime
is the update fragility, and even that is survivable at $10\times$ reduced LR --- but strictly
dominated: BC+Dirichlet at LR $10^{-5}$ reaches gauntlet 0.771 with median KL 0.102 (still
$2$--$3\times$ over target), vs.\ BC+categorical at LR $10^{-4}$ reaching 0.812 with KL 0.035 and
Elo $+161$ vs.\ $-9$ over the same 40 iterations. You can pay for the Dirichlet with a $10\times$
smaller trust region; there is no reason to.

\paragraph{Mechanism, quantified (CPU probe, \cd{scripts/probe\_dist\_sensitivity.py}).}
Over $E{=}48$ logit rows, sampled actions from each distribution, identical logits: the Dirichlet's
$\|\partial \log\pi(a)/\partial \ell\|_2$ is $5$--$6\times$ the categorical's at every sharpness
regime, and the log-prob movement per unit logit perturbation is $5$--$10\times$ --- which
compounds quadratically in KL, matching the $\sim$$100\times$ KL gap measured between the real
runs. The decisive asymmetry appears at BC sharpness: the \emph{categorical's} gradient on
confident samples collapses to $\approx 0$ (a sharp categorical is self-stabilizing), while the
Dirichlet's stays $\approx 5$ \emph{forever} --- it cannot settle, because suppressed components
sit at $\alpha = \softplus(-6) \approx 0.003 < 1$, where the density is \emph{singular} at the
simplex corner that every sample occupies; the $(\alpha_d{-}1)\log x_d$ terms keep injecting
$|\log x_d| \approx 14$-scale gradients regardless of how converged the policy is.

\section{Entropy bonus: gate-only}\label{v7:ent}
The v6 bonus used $H(\text{Bern}(p_s)) + p_s H(\text{Dir}(\alpha_s))$. Differential entropy of a
peaked Dirichlet is unboundedly \emph{negative}: after BC it measured $\approx -4{,}000$ per source,
so with coefficient $0.005$ the ``bonus'' became a $+20$ loss term whose gradient actively blurred
the BC-learned WHERE head (measured at warmup-end: grad-norm $1380$, KL $4.8$, Elo
$1032\!\to\!955$). v7 keeps only bounded terms: the gate Bernoulli entropy
($\le \ln 2$), plus the categorical WHERE entropy ($\le \ln E$) weighted by
\cd{ENT\_DIR\_COEF} (default $0$). Exploration is supplied by the stochastic gate
(\cd{GATE\_EPS} keeps $p\in[\varepsilon,1{-}\varepsilon]$), categorical sampling, and PFSP opponent
diversity --- not by fighting the BC sharpness.

\section{PPO guardrails}\label{v7:guard}

Three additions to the v6 update (\vsixref{ppo}); all are off-by-default-compatible knobs in the
config cell, defaults chosen for the warm-started regime:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Per-minibatch KL hard stop} (\cd{KL\_STOP\_MINIBATCH}, default on). The update
\emph{breaks mid-epoch} the moment a minibatch's $k_3$ KL estimate exceeds
$\cd{KL\_HARD\_MULT}\,(1.5)\times \cd{KL\_TARGET}\,(0.05)$. The v6 end-of-epoch check provably
cannot prevent the iteration-1 slam ($\text{KL}=2.06$ happened \emph{within} one epoch).
\item \textbf{Linear LR warmup} (\cd{LR\_WARMUP\_ITERS}): $\eta_t = \eta \cdot
\min(1, (t{+}1)/W)$ over the first $W$ iterations \emph{of a \cd{train()} call} (not global iters),
so every fresh-Adam phase (from-scratch, post-BC, resumed-with-fresh-moments) ramps in.
\item \textbf{Critic-only warmup} (\cd{VALUE\_WARMUP\_ITERS}): for the first $K$ iterations the
policy-surrogate and entropy terms are multiplied by 0 \emph{and only \cd{val\_*} parameters are
stepped}. The second clause is load-bearing and was measured the hard way: with a shared trunk, the
value loss alone (backpropagating into the trunk) dragged the BC policy by KL $0.15$--$0.84$ per
iteration with the policy surrogate completely off. Rationale for the warmup itself: after BC the
actor is strong but the critic is random; advantages computed against a random baseline are
structured noise, which is exactly what slammed previous warm starts. With the val-only restriction
the warmup iterations measure KL $\equiv 0.000$, then the actor unfreezes against a usable
baseline.
\end{enumerate}

entropy $0.005\!\to\!0.002$), fixed-opponent gauntlet (starter/intermediate/greedy, 64 envs each)
every 10 iterations for best-checkpoint selection.

\section{The A100 run}\label{v7:a100}
\cd{notebooks/setup1\_v7\_a100.ipynb} (generated by \cd{scripts/make\_a100\_nb.py} from the
patched v6 notebook, so it carries every fix in this document). Spec:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Model}: $d{=}768$, $L{=}6$ transformer (43.8M params; 127\,ms/move on the Kaggle
CPU --- comfortably deployable). \textbf{Batch}: $B{=}256$ envs $\times$ $T{=}500$ (128k
transitions/iter, $4\times$ the ladder legs). \textbf{Iters}: 600 (additional calls resume via
\cd{TRAIN\_STATE\_PATH}).
\item \textbf{Recipe}: \cd{BC\_ENABLED=True} (Run-All chains BC $\to$ Elo calibration $\to$
guardrailed PPO), \cd{WHERE\_DIST='categorical'}, gate-only entropy ($0.005\!\to\!0.002$),
\cd{VALUE\_WARMUP\_ITERS=5}, \cd{LR\_WARMUP\_ITERS=8}, per-minibatch KL stop, PFSP league with
snapshots every 25 iters, gauntlet best-ckpt every 25 iters, Elo re-grounding every 500.
\item \textbf{Projected cost}: simulator-bound scaling from the M leg gives $\approx$30\,s/iter
at $B{=}256$ on the A100 ($\approx$3$\times$ device factor on the element-wise-heavy env step);
600 iters $+$ 24 gauntlets $\approx$ \textbf{6\,h}, one Colab session. VRAM $\approx$5\,GB of
rollout buffers $+$ model/optimizer --- far inside 40\,GB; $B{=}512$ is the next lever if
throughput disappoints.
\item \textbf{Health signature to watch} (from the ladder): iters 1--5 KL $\equiv 0.000$; one
transient at unfreeze; then KL $\in[0.02, 0.06]$, Elo $+\sim$25/10\,iters, gauntlet $\ge$ BC level
($\approx$0.8) and rising. Any return of KL spikes $>1$ or a falling gauntlet means the

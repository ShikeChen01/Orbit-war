\begin{abstract}
\noindent This is document~3 of four. GRPO (\cd{cfg.ALGO="grpo"}) is a \emph{critic-free} PPO variant:
it replaces the learned value baseline with a \textbf{group baseline} (several rollouts share one
initial world; an advantage is the rollout's return relative to its group). It assumes document~1
(\cd{v12_setup.tex}) for the game, rollout, encoding, action, and reward, and reuses PPO's clipped
surrogate from document~2 verbatim. This document is precise about (i) how the group rollout is
rendered, (ii) the family of advantage estimators and their \emph{mutually exclusive} precedence,
(iii) the update. \textbf{It also records the empirical result that matters most}: every
``anti-collapse'' estimator in that family caused passivity in controlled ablation, and the keeper
is the \emph{legacy} per-group-std baseline with symmetric outcome decay.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why group-relative, and what changes vs PPO}
PPO's critic estimates a per-state baseline $V(s)$; GRPO instead rolls each world out $G$ times and
uses the \emph{within-group} return statistics as the baseline, so the world's intrinsic difficulty
cancels without a learned value function. Concretely, versus \cd{ppo_update}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{no value loss} --- the critic head is left untrained and unused (unless the
        \cd{GRPO_AUX_VALUE} representation aux is on);
  \item \textbf{no bootstrap} --- there is no critic to bootstrap from at the horizon;
  \item \textbf{optional KL-to-reference} (DeepSeekMath $\beta=\cd{GRPO_KL_COEF}$) against a frozen
        snapshot of the policy.
\end{itemize}
Everything else --- the gated-allocation actor, the clipped surrogate, entropy bonus, KL early-stop,
grad clipping, the GPU stat accumulators --- is shared with PPO. PPO and GRPO checkpoints are
interchangeable (the critic is simply dormant under GRPO). The advantage is precomputed in
\cd{collect_ppo} and stored in \cd{tb["advantage"]}, exactly like PPO, so \cd{grpo_update} just
consumes it.

%==================================================================================================
\section{The group rollout (\texttt{collect\_ppo}, GRPO branch)}
When \cd{cfg.ALGO="grpo"}, the rollout tiles $n_W$ distinct worlds into contiguous groups of $G$
envs (one group $=$ one world rolled out $G$ times), with $G=\cd{GRPO_GROUP}$ or \cd{GROUP_SIZE}
(falling back to the whole batch if $B\bmod G\ne0$). Each group is assigned a \emph{single} seat
geometry, cycling the seat rotations across groups so the ego still trains on every seat without
breaking within-group homogeneity (which the baseline depends on). The per-group return is
\[
  R^e = \begin{cases}
    O^e/\cd{PPO_REWARD_SCALE} & \cd{GRPO_OUTCOME_ONLY}\ \text{(terminal outcome only)},\\
    \sum_t r^e_t\,\cd{alive}^e_t & \text{otherwise (the full shaped return)},
  \end{cases}
\]
reshaped to $\R^{n_W\times G}$ as \cd{Rg}. \textbf{[D] \cd{GRPO_CRN}} optionally shares the
\emph{opponent's} sampling noise across the $G$ rollouts of a group (common random numbers; the ego
keeps independent noise) so the group baseline isolates ego-action variance.

\section{The advantage-estimator family}\label{sec:family}
The single most important structural fact: \textbf{the advantage estimators are mutually exclusive}.
Turning several on does \emph{not} stack them --- the code silently selects exactly one by
precedence. \cd{grpo_adv_mode(cfg,n_players)} resolves the active estimator and the
enabled-but-ignored ones, and \cd{train.py} prints the active choice at start with a loud warning
listing any ignored estimators. The precedence is
\[
  \text{phi-value\,[C]} \succ \text{sibling\,[B1]} \succ \text{analytic\,[A]\,(2p)}
  \succ \text{rank\,[C1]} \succ \text{CVaR\,[C2]} \succ \text{center/std\,[B]}.
\]
This footgun is exactly why an ``all flags on'' run collapses on an \emph{un-validated} per-step path
(it runs sibling or phi-value, never rank). Orthogonal levers (next subsection) \emph{do} compose.

\subsection{The flat estimators (\texttt{grpo\_flat\_advantage})}
One scalar advantage per rollout, broadcast over its steps. With $R^e$ as above:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{[B] legacy / Dr.GRPO / RLOO (the default ladder).} Centre on the group mean
        $\mu=\frac1G\sum_g R_g$ (or the leave-one-out mean if \cd{GRPO_LOO}). Then
        \cd{GRPO_STD_NORM=True} (default) divides by the per-group std,
        $A=(R-\mu)/(\sigma_g+\cd{GRPO_ADV_EPS})$; \cd{GRPO_STD_NORM=False} is Dr.GRPO --- centre-only
        with \emph{one global} scale (removes the cross-group difficulty-inversion bias of std-norm).
  \item \textbf{[A] analytic 2p binary} (\cd{GRPO_ANALYTIC_ADV}, 2p only). At a Beta-shrunk win-rate
        $\hat p=(\#\text{wins}+\alpha_0)/(G+2\alpha_0)$, the closed-form standardized advantage of a
        binary outcome: $A=\text{win}\,\sqrt{(1{-}\hat p)/\hat p}-\text{loss}\,\sqrt{\hat p/(1{-}\hat p)}$.
        \emph{Not} anti-collapse --- it has the same unbounded $\sqrt{(1{-}p)/p}$ as a rare win.
  \item \textbf{[C1] rank} (\cd{GRPO_RANK_ADV}). Van der Waerden / NES average-rank score: with
        average rank $\in[0,G{-}1]$, $A=(\text{rank}-\frac{G-1}{2})/\sqrt{(G^2-1)/12}$, bounded in
        $[-\sqrt3,\sqrt3]$ and invariant to every reward-magnitude knob.
  \item \textbf{[C2] CVaR} (\cd{GRPO_CVAR_ALPHA}$>0$). Risk-sensitive: baseline on the worst-$\alpha$
        tail quantile, with extra gradient weight \cd{GRPO_CVAR_LAMBDA} on the tail rollouts.
\end{itemize}
$[A]$, $[C1]$, and $[B]$-with-std are already $\sim$unit-variance and are passed through unscaled;
$[B]$-center-only and $[C2]$ get one global scale (or \cd{ADV_TARGET_RMS}, below).

\subsection{The per-step estimators (critic-free GAE)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{[B1] sibling} (\cd{GRPO_SIBLING_BASELINE}, \cd{_grpo_sibling_advantage}). The $G$
        shared-root rollouts of a group are bucketed by quantized $\Phi_{\text{ship}}(s_t)$ at each
        step; the matched-bucket mean terminal return is a per-step value $\hat V(s_t)$ (whole-group
        mean when a bucket is thinner than \cd{GRPO_SIBLING_MIN_PEERS}); then GAE$(\gamma,\lambda)$ on
        the per-step reward. A VinePPO-style per-step credit with no critic.
  \item \textbf{[C] phi-value} (\cd{GRPO_PHI_VALUE}, needs \cd{USE_POTENTIAL_SHAPING}). Build a value
        $\hat V(s_t)=\mu_g+a\,(\Phi(s_t)-\Phi(s_0))$ where $\mu_g$ is the group (or LOO) baseline and
        $a$ is the OLS slope of the outcome on the terminal $\Delta\Phi$, clamped to
        $[0,\cd{GRPO_PHI_A_MAX}]$; then GAE on the per-step reward. Here $\Phi$ is moved \emph{out} of
        the reward (\cd{dense_t}$=0$) and \emph{into} the value, turning shaping (which the group mean
        would cancel) into a per-step baseline.
\end{itemize}

\section{The update (\texttt{grpo\_update})}
\cd{grpo_update} runs the same \cd{UPDATE_EPOCHS}$\times$\cd{MINIBATCHES} clipped-surrogate passes as
PPO (document~2: the ratio, \cd{CLIP}/\cd{CLIP_HI}, per-owned-source entropy bonus, k3 KL early-stop,
grad clip, single end-of-update host sync), with four differences:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{no value loss}: $L=L^{\text{pol}}-\cd{ent_coef}\,\bar H$ (the policy is always at full
        weight --- \cd{policy_coef} is accepted for call-site parity but there is no critic to warm up);
  \item \textbf{KL-to-reference} (when \cd{GRPO_KL_COEF}$>0$ and a frozen \cd{ref_net} exists):
        $+\beta\,\widehat{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\Vert\pi_{\text{ref}})$ with the unbiased k3 estimator,
        gradient flowing through $\log\pi$. It is logged in the \cd{vf} slot (no critic) so the
        heartbeat prints unchanged;
  \item \textbf{[B2] aux value} (when \cd{GRPO_AUX_VALUE}): an MSE of the (otherwise unused) value head
        to the MC return, weighted by \cd{GRPO_AUX_COEF}, as a shared-trunk regularizer --- it does
        \emph{not} enter the critic-free advantage and keeps the weights PPO-compatible.
  \item \textbf{aux head} (when \cd{AUX_WIN_BET} or \cd{AUX_REWARD_PRED}): the same shallow scalar head's
        win-bet ($-\,b_t z$, $b_t=\tanh$) or reward-prediction (MSE) loss as PPO (document~2) is added
        here too, so the critic-free path --- including \cd{ALGO="mc"} --- trains it (representation aux only).
\end{itemize}

\section{Empirical verdict: the estimators failed; legacy is the keeper}\label{sec:verdict}
\paragraph{The original symptom.} Legacy GRPO (\cd{GRPO_STD_NORM=True}) was observed to death-spiral
in self-play (\cd{it426}$\to$\cd{it526}: launch/step $0.88\to0.37$, learner elo2 $2124\to1772$,
losing to its own earlier snapshot and to the \cd{greedy} watchdog). The advantage-bound probe
(\cd{grpo_diag.advantage_bound_probe}) showed that as the snapshot pool strengthens, the learner
faces \emph{lopsided losing groups} where a rare win's per-group-std advantage is
$\sqrt{(1{-}p)/p}$ --- unbounded as $p\to0$. The hypothesis was that the policy chases those lucky
wins into a passivity ratchet, and that a \emph{bounded, scale-invariant} estimator (rank) would fix
it.

\paragraph{The controlled result.} Three matched 3070\,Ti A/Bs (\cd{scripts/_gpu_ab_anticollapse.py},
\cd{_gpu_ab2.py}, \cd{_gpu_ab3.py}; h256 trunk, BC-warm, 2p, 100 iters, shared init/seed) reversed the
hypothesis:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccL{0.30\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
config & launch/step & elo2 & verdict \\
\midrule
\textbf{legacy std-norm} & $0.70\to0.59$ & $1497\to\mathbf{1576}$ & \textbf{stable / improving} \\
rank\,[C1] $+$ clip (the shipped default!) & $0.53\to0.10$ & $\to1259$ & passivity collapse \\
rank-noclip                                & $0.48\to0.27$ & $\to1292$ & passivity \\
Dr.GRPO\,[B] center-only                   & $0.57\to0.34$ & $\to1370$ & passivity \\
Dr.GRPO $+$ clip-hi                        & $0.46\to0.03$ & $\to1274$ & worst \\
sibling\,[B1] (an ``all-on'' path)         & $0.57\to0.24$ & $\to1295$ & passivity ($|A|_{\max}\,16$) \\
phi-value\,[C] (the other ``all-on'' path) & $0.59\to0.31$ & $\to1358$ & passivity ($|A|_{\max}\,22$) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{The cause, and a footgun.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{The collapse is the estimator change itself.} Every Tier~0--2 estimator (rank, with or
        without clip; Dr.GRPO center-only; sibling; phi-value) drives launch-rate and Elo down, while
        the legacy per-group std-norm baseline is stable and improving on the SAME shared init. The
        reward decay is \emph{not} a factor: an earlier hypothesis that an asymmetric ``lose-slower''
        decay triggered the collapse did not hold up.
  \item \textbf{The ``all flags on'' A100 collapse was also a precedence footgun.} With every flag on,
        \cd{grpo_adv_mode} selects sibling\,[B1] or phi-value\,[C] --- never rank --- and those
        per-step paths both go passive \emph{and} throw heavy-tailed advantages ($|A|_{\max}$ 16--22).
        The guard (active-estimator log $+$ ignored-estimator warning) was added so this is visible.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Why ``bounded $\ne$ good''.} The $\sqrt3$ rank bound held exactly --- but a rank advantage
discards win \emph{magnitude}, so it stops rewarding decisive aggression and settles into the
passivity basin (the project's oldest failure mode). The std-norm rare-win amplification the probe
flagged is double-edged: it is also what \emph{keeps} the agent aggressive. Removing the amplifier
removed the aggression.

\paragraph{The keeper, and the real next lever.} Train GRPO with
\[
  \boxed{\;\cd{GRPO_STD_NORM=True},\ \text{every estimator flag off},\ \cd{CLIP_HI=0}\;}
\]
If the \cd{it426}-style spiral recurs under the keeper, the evidence points to a
\textbf{trust-region / entropy} lever (KL-to-reference \cd{GRPO_KL_COEF}$>0$, or an entropy floor) on
top of legacy std-norm --- \emph{not} an advantage-estimator swap. The meta-lesson, now in the
project memory (\cd{grpo-anticollapse-v12}): a deterministic proof of a \emph{property}
(boundedness) is not a proof of \emph{improving the objective}; always local-ablate on the 3070\,Ti
before spending A100 time.

\paragraph{Diagnostic harness.} \cd{grpo_diag.py} holds \cd{advantage_bound_probe} (deterministic CPU
bound $+$ difficulty-inversion check), \cd{run_anticollapse_compare} (the 3-arm A/B, with
\cd{resume_from} to reproduce the real spiral on GPU), and \cd{plot_anticollapse}. The
\cd{ANTICOLLAPSE} / \cd{DRGRPO_BUNDLE} dicts in that module are a \emph{research harness that
empirically failed}, not a recommended configuration. The rollout exposes \cd{adv_absmax} /
\cd{adv_std} (and an \cd{aMx} heartbeat field) so a heavy-tailed advantage is visible live.
